How Your Data Is Protected

AI PrivacyShield actively monitors all outbound traffic to external AI models like Gemini and GPT. Every prompt and file is scanned for Personally Identifiable Information (PII) — including phone numbers, email addresses, Aadhaar numbers, passwords, credit cards, and API keys.


Detected sensitive information is automatically masked before transmission. Files containing direct PII are encrypted with AES-256 on local storage. You get the full power of AI without compromising your privacy.
